# **App Name**: Costa del Sol Navigator

## Core Features:

- Destination Guides: Display destination guides for various towns and cities within Costa del Sol, including overviews, things to do, and local insights.
- Hotel Silo Pages: Provide hotel silo pages with curated lists of accommodations, including luxury, family-friendly, and beachfront options. Allows users to check prices via Booking.com.
- Niche Interest Pages: Offer niche interest silo pages focusing on specific activities, such as golfing, with lists of golf courses and nearby hotels.
- Personalized Trip Planner: Integrate an AI tool which generates personalized trip plans based on user responses to a quiz, including town recommendations, activities, and ideal accommodations.
- Blog and Articles: Show blog and article pages that presents curated articles, guides, and stories related to Costa del Sol, including SEO optimization and social sharing options.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Navy (#003049) for main body text, structural elements, and headlines. This dark color evokes trustworthiness.
- Background color: Cream (#FDF0D5) as the dominant background to create a clean, minimalist aesthetic.
- Accent color: Red (#C1121F) for primary Call-to-Action buttons and key interactive elements to highlight conversion points. The brightness provides good contrast.
- Headline font: 'Anton' or 'Montserrat ExtraBold' (sans-serif) for large, impactful H1 titles.
- Body font: 'Inter' (sans-serif) for high legibility in paragraphs and descriptions.
- Use clean, minimalist icons to represent different categories and features.
- Employ a responsive 12-column grid system to ensure content reflows elegantly across desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints.
